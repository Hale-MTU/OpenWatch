# OpenWatch 0.5.0

Released 2026-11-24.

The Wear OS watch app is new from the dashboard down: tap a figure for its day, its week and last night; log water, weight, blood pressure and more from the wrist or a tile; measure heart rate, HRV, blood oxygen or air pressure on the spot; and run a workout with heart rate zones, auto-pause, a recovery measurement and a summary that matches what the phone writes to Health Connect. What you log on the watch reaches Health Connect, and the phone sends the watch your sleep, resting heart rate, energy and distance. The phone app pairs standard Bluetooth heart rate straps, weight scales, blood pressure monitors and glucose meters, and is complete in Arabic and Hebrew. Update the phone and the watch together: they now speak a new version of their link, and an older one asks to be updated.

### Added

- **A metric's own screen on the watch.** Tapping a dashboard tile opens today's chart, its low, average and high, the last days and, for the heart rate, the time in each zone and a live reading. Last night's HRV, blood oxygen, skin temperature and breathing rate are metrics of their own, counted by night, and the sleep screen shows last night's stages.
- **The quick log.** Water, weight, blood pressure, body fat, blood oxygen, breathing rate, body temperature, blood glucose and height, picked with the crown, and a one-minute breathing exercise. Choose which entries it shows and in which order. Each sync writes them to Health Connect as entered by hand; one whose access is off waits on the watch while the others are written.
- **Spot measurements.** Heart rate, a three-minute HRV, air pressure with its altitude, and on a Samsung watch blood oxygen, from the dashboard or a metric's screen. Progress runs along the edge of the screen and the wrist buzzes at the end; the result is kept as a measured entry.
- **Two new tiles and a tile editor.** "OpenWatch Today" shows today's steps and heart rate; "OpenWatch Quick log" adds a glass of water with a tap and opens the quick log or a heart rate measurement. Choose and order the dashboard's tiles, and add last night's metrics once the watch holds a reading.
- **Watch face complications** for heart rate, steps (also as a ring towards 8,000) and floors.
- **Workouts, rebuilt.** Pages to swipe between the controls, the overview, the heart rate zones and the pace or speed; the heart rate every second while a workout runs; auto-pause for running and cycling with GPS; a summary with the average and highest heart rate, the time in each zone, the training load, energy, pace, kilometre splits and VO2 max. After Stop the watch offers to measure the heart rate's recovery for two minutes, or reads it from a rest you already took, and says how far to trust it.
- **Workout detection.** After ten minutes of brisk walking or running the watch offers to record it, starting from when you began.
- **The phone's figures on the watch.** After each sync the watch shows your sleep with its stages, resting heart rate, active calories and distance for the last days, and the heart rate zones and body profile the phone keeps.
- **Workouts in Health Connect** now carry their kilometre laps, a line with their figures and a VO2 max reading, and their calories come from the same estimate the watch shows.
- **Settings on the watch.** The phone and its link, which sensor access is granted with a way to ask again, metric or imperial units, and the recording settings, each with a line that says what it does. The same lines are on the phone's watch card.
- **Standard Bluetooth devices on the phone.** A heart rate strap shows its live heart rate and writes one record a minute while it is collected; a weight scale, a blood pressure monitor and a glucose meter on the standard profiles hand over their readings when they wake. They are added under "Other devices". They were built against the Bluetooth profiles and have not yet met many real devices: tell us what yours does.
- **Arabic and Hebrew.** The phone app is complete in both, and the watch app in every language of the phone app.
- **A wide window** shows the device list beside the device you opened.

### Changed

- **Update both apps together.** The phone and the watch speak version 5 of their link, which asks for every kind of data the same way. A 0.4 watch or phone is told to update instead of syncing nothing.
- **New Health Connect permissions.** To write what you log on the watch and what a standard device measures, the phone app asks to write hydration, mindfulness, body temperature, height, blood pressure and blood glucose, and to read active calories and distance for the figures it sends the watch. Each is listed, with its reason, on the "What OpenWatch reads and writes" screen; syncs carry on without any of them.
- **Skin temperature waits for your baseline.** The first three nights a watch records are no longer written as "no change"; they form your own baseline, and the watch's card on the phone says how many nights are still needed.
- **Breathing rate is written by default,** and the watch's night is written beside another app's night, with Health Connect's priority deciding which one counts. A switch in Settings turns the second off.
- **The watch's sleep hold** is synced with the heart rate by default where the watch's heart rate sensor can wake it, instead of holding the processor awake all night; Do Not Disturb counts as bedtime only between 21:00 and 09:00.
- **Home** no longer shows the watch a second time above the device list.
- **Steps count live** while the watch app is on screen, and a running measurement or workout dims to an ambient view instead of keeping the screen lit.

### Fixed

- **The live heart rate blocked the watch's status.** While the device screen showed the live heart rate, the status check and "Validate" said "No answer"; the phone now takes turns on the watch's one link.
- **A long workout's route was cut short;** it is now thinned to fit, so it keeps its end.
- **Unpulled data could be pruned without warning.** The watch's status shows when a phone last fetched its data, and a notification warns two days before data the phone has not fetched would be removed.
- **Fixes:** a failed read shows as a failure in your language instead of as nothing; the add flows and the send-point form survive Android reclaiming the app; a pace too slow to mean anything shows a dash; a night that reaches the phone in two syncs counts once in the skin temperature baseline; long lists and dialogs leave room for the keyboard and a maximum width on large screens.

The phone app and the Wear OS app share the package name `tech.mmarca.openwatch` and the signing certificate, so both install as a normal update; the watch's versionCode is 2000000000 plus the phone's.

Distribution flow:

- GitHub release with the signed phone APK, signed debug APK, signed Wear OS APK and both signed Android App Bundle assets.
- The Google Play update follows later: Play uploads are held while the store's health data declaration is brought up to date for the new permissions.
