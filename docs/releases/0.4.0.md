# OpenWatch 0.4.0

Released 2026-11-15.

The Wear OS watch app now does more on its own. It opens on today's dashboard, records a workout with GPS, heart rate, distance and climb, and at night measures heart rate variability and, on a Samsung watch, blood oxygen and skin temperature. The phone writes all of it to Health Connect: each workout with its route and an estimate of its active calories, and naps as sleep sessions of their own. OpenWatch now takes its calculations from the library it shares with OpenVitals, so both apps give the same maximum heart rate and the same calorie estimate from the same data. The Connected apps screen is harder to trick, and the phone app's screens are translated into all thirteen languages again.

### Added

- **Today's dashboard on the watch.** The watch app opens on today's steps and heart rate as the watch recorded them, with Start activity and Settings, where the status screen now lives. Tapping a metric opens nothing yet.
- **Workouts recorded on the watch.** Start activity offers 18 sports, from walking, running, hiking and cycling to strength, yoga and rowing, and shows the time, heart rate, distance, steps and climb, with pause, resume and stop. The heart rate runs at full rate during a workout, and the GPS is used only by sports with a route. The phone writes each workout to Health Connect as an exercise session with its route and its pauses, and with its distance when the watch measured one; the steps, climb and heart rate are already in the all-day records. Without the exercise, route or distance permission the workouts wait on the watch, and the sync card says which permission is missing.
- **Active calories for a watch workout.** Each workout the phone writes gets an active calories record, estimated as OpenVitals does: from the watch's heart rate outside the pauses (Keytel et al. 2005) while it lies between 57 and 90 % of your maximum, else from the distance for a walk or run at a pace ACSM's metabolic equations cover, else from the sport's 2024 Adult Compendium MET, with the resting share taken off. Without a weight in the body profile there is no estimate, and the sync card says so; a missing permission skips the calories, never the workout.
- **Heart rate variability at night.** While sleep is held and the arm has been still for ten minutes, the watch measures HRV (RMSSD) in short windows of its raw PPG sensor, and the phone writes each window to Health Connect. A watch without the raw sensor offers none.
- **Breathing rate, blood oxygen and skin temperature at night.** Each HRV window also gives a breathing rate, which the phone writes only when Settings, Breathing rate from the watch is on; it is off by default and experimental. On a watch with the Samsung Health Platform, the watch can take SpO2 at most once an hour while you sleep and still, and record the skin temperature through the night, each behind its own switch, off by default. The phone writes the skin temperature as one record per night, as a change from your earlier nights.
- **The Samsung Health Platform as a source.** On a Samsung watch the heart rate and the HRV windows can come from the platform instead of the raw sensors, chosen on the watch's status screen. The platform's HRV needs its Developer Mode, which the screen says. After an error or five silent minutes, OpenWatch falls back to the watch's own sensor.
- **Naps.** Sleep outside the main night is written as its own sleep session.
- **A night recorded without bedtime mode.** After ten still minutes on the wrist with a falling heart rate (twenty by day), the watch keeps recording every minute as it does in bedtime mode, and stops after five waking minutes, off the wrist, on the charger or after ten hours. Unlocking the phone ends such a hold and keeps a new one from starting for thirty minutes.
- **Power-save heart rate.** The watch measures the heart rate every 10 seconds or, in power save, about every 10 minutes outside bedtime mode; bedtime mode always measures every 10 seconds.
- **Watch settings from the phone.** The phone's watch screen has a Watch settings card that reads the watch's own settings and changes them: the heart rate plan and source, the HRV source, the sleep hold, skin temperature and SpO2, with the Samsung switches only where the watch has the platform and a hint when a permission is missing on the watch.

### Changed

- **One set of calculations with OpenVitals.** The calculations OpenWatch shares with OpenVitals come from one library, so both apps give the same number from the same data. With it come OpenVitals' latest formulas: one maximum heart rate for every estimate, the one you entered, else the highest reading of the year before, else the age formula; the heart rate weighted by time in a workout's calories; and, without a sex in the body profile, the mean of the men's and women's formulas.
- **Calories for an imported workout.** A workout file that recorded no calories gets the estimate OpenVitals' entry form makes, from the body profile and the heart rate the file holds, for every sport the 2024 Adult Compendium covers, not only those with a GPS route. A fixed 70 kg is no longer assumed: without a weight there is no estimate, and the Garmin sync says so.
- **Adding a Wear OS watch needs Android's association.** A watch is added only when you allow Android's companion dialog, which a workout started on the watch will need. A watch added before, or whose association was removed in Android's settings, shows "Link watch" on its status card. A Garmin watch keeps the optional association.
- **Connected apps is harder to trick.** An app that uses OpenVitals' name without being signed by the OpenVitals project gets a warning on its card, and OpenWatch trusts a first-party app by its name and key together, so a rotated key raises no warning. While the screen shows, touches through another app's window are dropped and, from Android 12, other apps' overlays are hidden, so nothing can be drawn over Allow or a switch. The home screen card for an app that asks to connect is gone, since any app could raise it; such an app still heads the Connected apps list.
- **The watch app stays offline.** It drops the network permissions, as the phone app does, and its status screen shows whether Samsung's diagnostics are on.
- **Languages.** The home, Health Connect setup, body profile and settings screens, and the titles and buttons of Connected apps, are translated into all thirteen languages, with this release's new lines. The watch app is in English, and in German.

### Fixed

- **Health Connect setup never finished.** OpenWatch asked for two permissions Health Connect does not define, steps cadence and cycling pedaling cadence, so setup always reported one missing and, once every other permission was granted, the permission dialog did not open. The cadences are written under the steps and exercise permissions.

The phone app and the Wear OS app share the package name `tech.mmarca.openwatch` and the signing certificate, so both install as a normal update; the watch's versionCode is 2000000000 plus the phone's.

Distribution flow:

- GitHub release with the signed phone APK, signed debug APK, signed Wear OS APK and both signed Android App Bundle assets.
- Direct Google Play production upload, to the phone track and the Wear OS track, from the approved production run of the release workflow.
